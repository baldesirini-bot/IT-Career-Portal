Stay safe online while you learn

Before you make an account:
  • Some sites have an age limit, often 13 or older. Ask a parent or teacher first.
  • Use a strong, unique password for each site. Three or four random words works well.
  • Never share your real password, ID number or bank details with anyone.
  • If a message says “act now or lose your account,” it’s probably a scam.
Free vs paid: Most learning on this page is free. Some certificates cost money. You never need to pay to start.
